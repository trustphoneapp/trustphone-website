TrustPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

Plain-language summary: TrustPhone is designed to perform its current security analysis locally on your Android device. Notification previews, APK file contents, installed-app information, and usage information are not uploaded to TrustPhone servers. TrustPhone does not currently operate a cloud threat-intelligence service. The free tier may use Google AdMob, and purchases use Google Play Billing.

1. Who We Are

TrustPhone is an Android security-tools application provided by YS Ventures LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how TrustPhone accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

Questions can be sent to support@trustphone.app.

2. Local Security Analysis

TrustPhone provides tools that help you review warning signs and make informed decisions. TrustPhone does not guarantee that an app, file, website, sender, or notification is safe or unsafe.

Current security analysis runs locally on your device. TrustPhone does not upload notification text, APK file contents, installed-app inventory, or usage information to TrustPhone servers.

3. Optional Notification Access

If you enable Notification Access in Android settings, TrustPhone can review readable notification previews locally on your device to identify warning signs such as suspicious links, impersonated brands, repeated website alerts, and related context across messages from the same sender.

  • Notification Access is optional.
  • TrustPhone does not read your SMS inbox.
  • TrustPhone does not upload notification text.
  • TrustPhone does not automatically remove the original notification.

To explain warnings inside the app, TrustPhone may temporarily store a shortened notification snippet locally. Stored snippets are limited in length. If a link is detected, TrustPhone stores only the website domain needed to explain the warning sign, not the full URL or private query tokens.

4. Installed-App Information

TrustPhone reviews installed apps to show useful Android-reported facts, including app name, package name, installation source, requested permissions, and certain special-access settings.

Android treats installed-app inventory as sensitive information. TrustPhone uses this access only to provide its app-review features. Installed-app inventory is processed locally and is not uploaded to TrustPhone servers.

TrustPhone may also detect when an app is installed or removed so that you can review recent changes on your device.

5. Optional Usage Access

If you enable Usage Access in Android settings, TrustPhone uses aggregated device usage information locally to show recent app activity and richer storage estimates. This permission is optional. TrustPhone does not upload usage information to TrustPhone servers.

6. Optional Media and File Access

If you choose to grant media access, TrustPhone uses the files Android allows it to read to estimate storage categories on your device. TrustPhone can work with limited media access.

When you use the APK file-check feature, Android's file picker allows you to choose an APK file or an allowed folder. TrustPhone reads only the file or folder access that you select. APK analysis is performed locally. APK files are not uploaded to TrustPhone servers.

7. Sender-Source Checks

TrustPhone includes a small local registry of sender information for selected commonly impersonated brands. If a message claims to come from a known source, TrustPhone may compare the sender and website domain with locally stored reference information.

This is a helpful review signal, not cryptographic proof and not a guarantee. Sender-source checks run locally on your device.

8. Local Storage and Encryption

Warning history is stored locally in an encrypted SQLCipher database. The database passphrase is protected using Android Keystore AES-256-GCM.

  • Notification warning alerts auto-delete after 7 days.
  • Sender review history auto-deletes after 90 days.
  • Short-lived context used to connect related alerts is pruned after 30 minutes.
  • You can clear locally stored warning history from inside the app.
  • Uninstalling TrustPhone removes its locally stored app data.

9. Ads and Advertising ID

The free tier may display limited ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may process information such as the device advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information where permitted by Android, Google policies, and applicable law.

This information is handled by Google under its own policies. TrustPhone does not send notification text, installed-app inventory, APK file contents, or usage information to AdMob.

Learn more through Google's Privacy Policy .

10. Google Play Billing

Optional purchases, subscriptions, and donations are processed through Google Play Billing. Google may process purchase-related information under its own policies. TrustPhone does not receive your full payment-card details.

TrustPhone may receive the purchase status needed to unlock ad-free access or confirm an optional donation.

11. User-Initiated Sharing

Some app screens may allow you to share selected information using Android's share menu. Sharing occurs only when you choose it. Information you share is handled by the app or service you select and is subject to that service's privacy policy.

12. Information TrustPhone Does Not Request

TrustPhone does not currently request access to:

  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your SMS inbox
  • Your call history
  • Your precise location
  • Your microphone
  • Your camera
  • Accessibility Service control

13. Accounts and Deletion

TrustPhone does not currently require or provide user accounts. There is no TrustPhone cloud account to delete.

You can clear locally stored warning history inside the app. You can remove remaining locally stored TrustPhone data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

14. Future Optional Cloud Features

TrustPhone does not currently upload security-analysis data to a TrustPhone cloud service.

If TrustPhone introduces optional community threat-intelligence features in the future, those features will be disclosed clearly before use. Where required, they will be off by default and require your affirmative consent. This Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.

15. Website Privacy

The TrustPhone website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Standard connection information, such as IP address and browser request details, may be processed by the hosting provider when you visit this website.

This website currently loads Google Fonts. Loading those fonts may cause standard connection information, such as your IP address, to be sent to Google. The website does not require an account and does not use a TrustPhone analytics service.

Learn more through GitHub's Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy .

16. Children

TrustPhone is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a TrustPhone cloud account because TrustPhone does not currently provide accounts.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TrustPhone changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published. Material changes will be disclosed where required by applicable law or platform policy.
